Xiaopeng Zhang (张晓鹏)

Biography

I received my Ph.D. in 2022 from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), under the supervision of Prof. Evangeline F.Y. Young. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from Shandong University (SDU) and my master's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Beihang University (BUAA). My research spans physical design and design for manufacturability in VLSI CAD. My doctoral work focused on learning-based layout generation, while my industrial work focuses on routing automation, power integrity analysis for 3D ICs, and advanced packaging.
